There are several cards arranged in a row, and each card has an associated number of points.
In one step, you can take one card from the beginning or from the end of the row.You have to take exactly k cards.
Your score is the sum of the points of the cards you have taken.
Given the integer array cardsPoints and the integer k, return the maximum score you can obtain.

 Taking k cards from the ends means leaving n − k cards as one continuous subarray in the middle.
To maximize the picked sum, minimize the sum of the middle subarray of size n − k.
Use a sliding window to find the minimum sum subarray of length n − k.
The final answer is:
total sum of array − minimum middle window sum.
